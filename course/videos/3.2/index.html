Right type first, then a clean look.
Every value has a type.
Text is for names, units and remarks.
Numbers are counts you can add.
Currency is money, with the peso sign and two decimals.
Dates are days you can sort, so type them year, month, day.
And percentages are parts of a whole.
Quick test:
after you press Enter, a real number or date jumps
to the right side of the cell.
The same employee wears a different uniform on Monday and
on Friday, but it's still the same person.
A format changes how a value looks, never the value itself.
So don't type the peso sign or commas yourself.
Type 245, and let the format dress it up.
Now the trap. Two quantities sit on the left:
30 reams, with the unit typed in,
and a 10 that was copied in as text.
SUM adds C2 to C5 and shows 29,
but the real total is 69.
Text is skipped, and nobody is warned.
Retype them as plain numbers, or use Convert to Number,
and the total is right again.
Only mobile numbers and TINs stay as text,
to keep the zero.
Lab 3 needs at least one column formatted for its
data type, so let's do the money.
Select D2 to E9:
the unit costs and the empty Total Cost cells.
Press Control 1, choose Currency, set the symbol to the
Philippine peso with two decimal places,
then click OK.
In Google Sheets, it's Format, Number,
Custom currency.
Click D2: the cell shows pesos, but the formula bar still says
245.
Now make it readable.
Select row 1, press Control B,
centre it, and give it a dark fill with white text.
Wrap long headers like Reorder Level.
Keep numbers right-aligned, so the digits line up.
Add All Borders, because gridlines usually don't print.
Then freeze the top row, so the headers never scroll
away. And use Merge and Center only for a title above
the table.
Next, let the sheet warn you.
Select C2 to C9.
Go to Home, Conditional Formatting, New Rule,
and use a formula: equals C2, less than or equal to,
F2, with a light red fill.
In plain words: is this quantity at or below its reorder level?
Three cells turn red: Folder, Fastener, which is exactly at its level,
and Envelope.
Change a quantity, and the colour updates by itself.
The same skills work at home.
In your monthly household budget, format Budget and Actual as
Currency. Then select C2 to C9 and add a rule:
equals C2 greater than B2.
Overspending turns red.
Four items light up: rice, the
CEPALCO bill, fare and groceries. Now you know where to cut next month.
Before you calculate, clean any raw data copied from an old file.
Remove extra spaces with TRIM. Use one spelling per unit:
ream, not r m or capital Ream.
Control H finds and replaces.
Delete typed totals, because formulas will replace them.
And delete blank rows, which break shortcuts and totals.
Quick check.
The Qty total is too small, and some quantities sit on the left.
Why?
Is the column too narrow, do the cells need Currency
format, are the quantities stored as text,
or is the top row frozen? Think about it for a moment. The answer:
they're stored as text.
Totals skip text.
To recap: right type first, with numbers on the right and no
units or peso signs typed in. Then format:
currency, a bold header, borders, a frozen top row,
and a reorder warning in red.
And clean raw data before you calculate.

with formulas.
