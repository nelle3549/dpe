Visual ethics

Same numbers, different story

✅

Axis starts at 0

Honest
  • Bond paper, long: 7,950
  • Bond paper, A4: 6,125
  • About 30% apart
❌

Axis starts at 6,000

Misleading
  • Same numbers
  • Long bar looks 15× bigger
  • The picture lies
No fake images of real people. Label AI images: "Image: AI-generated".

Privacy · what you paste is shared

Never feed personal data to an AI tool

🏢

At the office

  • No taxpayer names, TINs, payroll or memos
  • Sample data only
  • Office-allowed tools only
🏠

At home

  • No GCash number or address on that poster
  • Think twice about kids' photos
Would you post it on a public bulletin board? If not, don't paste it.
