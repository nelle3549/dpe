File Management & Everyday AI

Plus the 10-item Digital Foundations Quiz at the end of 1.5.

  • A master folder with at least two clearly named sub-folders
  • One screenshot or downloaded image inside a sub-folder
  • One text document with a response from an AI assistant
Decks 1.2, 1.3 and 1.5 →
30%Lab 2 · 3.5 hours

AI-Assisted Word Processing & Mail Merge

Default: an invitation to a digital skills workshop.

  • Multi-paragraph letter with human edits, no "[Insert Name]"
  • Justified text, standard font sizes, 1-inch margins
  • A table of at least 2 columns x 3 rows
  • Mail merge: at least 3 personalised letters in one file
