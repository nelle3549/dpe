The big idea

An AI that reads only your sources.

  • Gemini Notebook is Google's notebook for your own files. Its old name was NotebookLM.
  • Sources: Google Docs and Slides, PDFs, Word files, web links, YouTube videos, pasted text.
  • Answers cite the source: click a number to see the exact sentence it used.
  • Studio turns the sources into a slide deck, a video, an audio talk, a quiz, flashcards and more.
  • Still a copilot: it can add things your source never said.

Microsoft Edge on a Mac, sample Google account. Any browser works: Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari.

Step 1 · Find it

Three ways in, one sign-in

  1. Type the address: notebook.google.com (the old notebooklm.google.com still works).
  2. Or search NotebookLM or Gemini Notebook. Click the result from google.com.
  3. Or open the Google apps menu (the 9 dots, top right of Gmail or Google) and click Notebook.
  4. Click Try Gemini Notebook and sign in with your Google account.

Sample report: DPE sample - Admin Section Year-End Report 2026, in the trainer's demo Drive. Sample source: Admin Section year-end report

Step 2 · Your source

One notebook per project

  1. On the home page, click New notebook.
  2. Upload files from your computer, or pick Drive. Links and copied text work too.
  3. In Drive, search the file name.
  4. Click the file, then Insert. It appears under Sources in a few seconds.

Question typed in the chat box: "What are the three biggest results this year? Give the numbers."

Ask first

Every number has a source

  1. Ask a plain question. The answer has small numbers after each fact.
  2. Click a number: the exact sentence from your report pops up. Compare it.
  3. Three panels: Sources (left), Chat (middle), Studio (right) for decks and videos.

Studio › Slide Deck › pencil icon (Customize). Clicking the tile itself starts at once with the defaults.

Step 3 · Studio

Slide Deck: say who it's for

  1. In Studio, click the pencil on Slide Deck to customize it.
  2. Detailed Deck (to read) or Presenter Slides (to talk over). Pick a length.
  3. Describe the deck: audience, number of slides, what to stress. Then Generate now.
  4. It takes about 5 minutes. You can keep chatting meanwhile.

Fix, then share

Revise one slide, keep the rest

  1. Open the deck large and click Revise.
  2. Pick the slide, type the change: "Remove the sentence about the cause. Keep only the numbers." Then Generate revised deck.
  3. Present right here, or share a link.
  4. ⋮ › Download as PDF or PowerPoint.
Good to know: in the PowerPoint download, each slide is one picture. For small text fixes, use Revise, or rebuild that one slide yourself.

What we got · then we checked it

Right numbers, a few extra words

  • All four results, the 1,240 memos, 120 offices, ₱22,039 across 8 items: correct.
  • "Reduces our environmental impact" and "frees up office space": not in the report.
  • Calls the AI rule "mandatory". The report says "adopt a simple rule".
  • Says AI tools "aren't invaluable". It meant "not perfect". Narration can misspeak.

Before you use it at work

Six rules for Gemini Notebook

Only shareable files

No names, addresses, ID numbers, salaries or case details of real people. Check your office's AI policy.

Ask, then generate

A question in the chat shows quickly whether it understood your source.

Click the citations

Every number on a slide should match a sentence in your source.

Hunt for extras

Causes, dates, praise and "mandatory" words the source never said. Fix them with Revise.

Mind the limits

Free accounts have a daily AI usage limit. Decks take minutes, videos longer.

You own it

Your name is on the report. Label AI-made media when you share it.
