The big idea

A formula is a calculator that remembers.

  • At the sari-sari store, the tindera punches the numbers, writes the total, and forgets how she got it.
  • A formula keeps the recipe: "quantity times unit cost". Change an ingredient and the answer changes too.
  • You see the answer in the cell and the recipe in the formula bar.

By the end of this topic

You will be able to…

  1. Write: start with =, use + − * / ^ and parentheses in the right order.
  2. Reference: use C2*D2 instead of typed numbers, then fill it down.
  3. Check: read #DIV/0!, #VALUE!, #NAME? and #####, and show all formulas.
Lab 3 rule: values must not be hard-typed. Every calculated cell must show a formula in the formula bar.

Rule number one

Every formula starts with an equals sign

Same in Excel, Sheets and Calc

Microsoft Excel for Mac, sample Lab3 Inventory. Same rule in Excel for Windows, Google Sheets and Calc.

  1. Without =: C2*D2 is just text. The cell shows exactly what you typed, on the left.
  2. With =: =C2*D2 says "calculate this". The cell shows ₱6,125.00; the formula bar keeps the recipe.
Fast and accurate: type =, then click C2, type *, click D2, press Enter. Clicking avoids typing the wrong address.

The most important habit

Point to cells. Don't type the numbers.

Microsoft Excel for Mac, sample Lab3 Inventory.

  1. Cell references: E2 holds =C2*D2 and shows ₱6,125.00.
  2. The supplier raises the A4 price: D2 becomes 255. E2 updates by itself to ₱6,375.00.
  3. Typed numbers: =25*245 still says ₱6,125.00. Wrong, and nobody is warned.
Why it matters at work: one price update fixes the row total, the grand total, and later the chart. With typed numbers, you'd fix every one by hand.

Build the Total Cost column

One formula in E2, then fill it down

Microsoft Excel for Mac, sample Lab3 Inventory.

  1. Click E2. Type =, click C2, type *, click D2, press Enter.
  2. Click E2 again. Double-click the fill handle (the small square at its corner), or drag it down to E9.
  3. Click E5 and read the formula bar: =C5*D5. The row number changed by itself.
Why it adjusts: the formula means "this row's Qty × this row's Unit Cost". More on this in 3.5 Cell Referencing.

At home · Monthly Household Budget

Budget vs Actual: difference and percentage

Microsoft Excel for Mac, sample Household Budget.

  1. Difference · D2: =C2-B2, filled down. Positive = over budget (CEPALCO ₱320.00). Negative = saved.
  2. % of Budget · E2: =C2/B2, filled down, then format as Percentage: 111.4% for CEPALCO. Don't multiply by 100.
Percentage format: Home › % (Percent Style), or Ctrl+1 › Percentage › 1 decimal place.

Formulas you'll actually use

The same four operators, everywhere

At the office
  • Supplies request: Qty × Unit Cost per line, then a grand total
  • Delivered vs requested: =C2-B2 shows what's still missing
  • Percent of a monthly target reached: =C2/B2 as %
  • Per-piece cost: box price ÷ pieces per box
At home
  • Budget vs Actual: difference and % of budget
  • Sari-sari profit: selling price − cost per piece
  • Fiesta food: guests × servings × cost per serving
  • School project shares: total cost ÷ number of classmates

Don't panic

Error messages tell you what went wrong

Microsoft Excel for Mac, sample sheet with errors made on purpose. The warning icon beside an error cell lists help for it.

  • #DIV/0! Divided by zero or an empty cell. Fix: fill in the divisor.
  • #VALUE! Math on text, like "30 reams". Fix: plain numbers only.
  • #NAME? A word the sheet doesn't know: =C2xD2 or =SUMM( ). Fix: use *; check spelling.
  • ##### Not an error: the column is too narrow. Fix: double-click the column border.
Find the cause: click the error cell and read the formula bar.

Check your whole sheet at once

Show formulas with Ctrl+`

Microsoft Excel for Mac (Formulas tab › Show Formulas; Ctrl+` on Windows).

  • Excel: Ctrl+` (the key left of 1), or Formulas › Show Formulas.
  • Caught! E5 shows a plain 2080: someone typed it by hand.
  • Google Sheets: Ctrl+`, or View › Show › Formulas.
  • LibreOffice Calc: View › Show Formula.
Press it again to go back to normal.
